# E4-S2: Customer 360 screen

- **ID:** E4-S2
- **Epic:** E4 - Customer 360 and Next-Best-Action
- **Layer:** UI
- **Slice:** 1
- **Group:** G
- **depends_on:** E4-S1, E3-S4, E6-S6

## Description

Build the Customer 360 screen that helps an officer understand a customer's collection situation, clearly distinguishing AI recommendations from deterministic data and showing escalation, hardship, dispute and simulated payment status.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The screen shows DPD, bucket, overdue amount, priority band with each contributing factor, active PTP or arrangement and recent interactions
2. AI-generated content is inside a panel with the text label 'AI-generated' and deterministic data panels carry the label 'Rules engine', with the distinction conveyed by text and icon and not color alone
3. Escalation status, hardship indicators and dispute status are visible and an account with an OPEN escalation shows the badge 'Escalated - human review'
4. Simulated payments in the history are labelled 'Simulated' and the page passes axe with zero serious or critical violations
5. Customer 360 main content is interactive in under 2 s p95 over 30 scripted loads after one warm-up
6. When the AI provider is unavailable the screen shows an 'AI unavailable - manual workflow available' state, fabricates no recommendation, and all deterministic data and manual actions remain usable; a STALE or UNKNOWN snapshot shows a stale-data banner with a refresh action
7. Keyboard navigation reaches every panel and control on the screen, and any dialog moves focus in on open, traps focus while open and returns focus to its trigger on close
8. A Record Promise-to-Pay form lets the officer record a customer commitment manually and works when the AI provider is unavailable
