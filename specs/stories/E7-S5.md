# E7-S5: Compliance review queue and decision capability

- **ID:** E7-S5
- **Epic:** E7 - Escalation and Human Review
- **Layer:** API
- **Slice:** 2
- **Group:** I
- **depends_on:** E7-S2

## Description

Route policy-exception and high-risk compliance cases to the COMPLIANCE_REVIEW queue and give COMPLIANCE_RISK the single narrow record_compliance_review_decision capability, without any ability to change financial or collections state.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. POLICY_EXCEPTION and HIGH_RISK_COMPLIANCE escalations, whether proposed by the AI or raised by a reviewer ESCALATE, are routed to the COMPLIANCE_REVIEW queue with reviewer role COMPLIANCE_RISK
2. COMPLIANCE_RISK can use record_compliance_review_decision on COMPLIANCE_REVIEW cases to record the review outcome, a mandatory reason, the reviewer persona, a timestamp and the case reference
3. The capability can transition only a case in COMPLIANCE_REVIEW according to the case state machine, and an attempt on a case in any other queue or state is refused
4. The compliance decision cannot modify balances, PaymentEvents, PTPs, arrangements, hardship data, dispute facts or customer and account financial state, verified by a before and after comparison of those tables around a decision
5. Every compliance decision is audited, and submitting the same decision with the same idempotency key returns the original result without repeating the transition
6. COLLECTIONS_OFFICER, COLLECTIONS_MANAGER and CUSTOMER receive 403 on the compliance decision endpoint
