# E6-S4: PTP lifecycle: KEPT and BROKEN transitions

- **ID:** E6-S4
- **Epic:** E6 - Customer Conversation, Promise-to-Pay and Simulated Payment
- **Layer:** Service
- **Slice:** 1
- **Group:** H
- **depends_on:** E6-S2, E6-S3

## Description

Implement deterministic PTP status transitions over the injected Clock using the PTP satisfaction rule: cumulative qualifying simulated payments reaching the promised amount by the due date move PENDING to KEPT, partial payments leave it PENDING, and a due date passing without satisfaction moves it to BROKEN, with a job that is safe to rerun (AC-A6, scenarios 25 and 26). The LLM performs none of this.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Successful simulated PaymentEvents whose cumulative qualifying amount reaches the promised amount on or before the due date move the PTP from PENDING to KEPT with an audit event, using the PTP satisfaction rule
2. A single successful payment below the promised amount leaves the PTP PENDING with the cumulative amount recorded, and a later payment that completes the promised amount by the due date moves it to KEPT
3. Failed payments, payments below the qualifying minimum and payments after the due date do not count toward KEPT
4. Running the breakage job after the due date when the cumulative qualifying amount is below the promised amount moves the PTP to BROKEN under the TestClock and does not touch PTPs due later
5. Running the breakage job twice, or replaying a payment event, does not re-transition a KEPT or BROKEN PTP or create duplicate audit events
6. A PTP moves only along PENDING to KEPT, BROKEN or CANCELLED and any other transition (for example KEPT to PENDING) is rejected
