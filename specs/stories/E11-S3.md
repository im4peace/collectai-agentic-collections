# E11-S3: Journey B2 end-to-end: financial hardship

- **ID:** E11-S3
- **Epic:** E11 - End-to-End Journey Verification
- **Layer:** Service
- **Slice:** 2
- **Group:** K
- **depends_on:** E8-S2, E7-S3, E9-S2

## Description

Automated end-to-end verification of Journey B2: hardship detection, structured capture, treatment suppression, escalation, human review and audit, including the vulnerable-customer variant.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. A Playwright test as the bound CUSTOMER describes job loss and the system records a structured hardship case, stops automated treatment and creates an escalation in HARDSHIP_REVIEW with elevated priority
2. As COLLECTIONS_OFFICER the case appears in the review queue, the officer records a decision with a reason, and the UI blocks submission without a reason
3. Customer 360 shows the hardship status and a human-review-only next-best-action, and no automated outreach is produced for the account
4. A vulnerable-customer variant with vulnerability_detected true creates a VULNERABLE_CUSTOMER_REVIEW escalation and the audit viewer shows the full chain
