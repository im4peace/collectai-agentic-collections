# E2-S4: Treatment suppression and contact-frequency policy

- **ID:** E2-S4
- **Epic:** E2 - Deterministic Rules Engine
- **Layer:** Service
- **Slice:** 1
- **Group:** B
- **depends_on:** E1-S1, E1-S2

## Description

Implement deterministic contact and treatment policy: maximum contact attempts, minimum interval, suppression for active disputes and hardship or vulnerable cases. These are simulated demo rules per BRD 13.3, not regulatory claims.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Contact is denied with reason MAX_ATTEMPTS when attempts in the period reach the configured maximum and allowed when one below
2. Contact is denied with reason MIN_INTERVAL when the last attempt is within the configured interval under the TestClock and allowed after it
3. An active dispute on an item suppresses automated recommendations for that item only, while other items on the account remain eligible
4. Active hardship or a vulnerable-customer flag suppresses all automated treatment for the account until cleared by a human decision
