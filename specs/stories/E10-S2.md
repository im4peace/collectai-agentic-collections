# E10-S2: Evaluation metrics and reporting rules

- **ID:** E10-S2
- **Epic:** E10 - Evaluation, Analytics and Portfolio Deliverables
- **Layer:** Service
- **Slice:** 1
- **Group:** I
- **depends_on:** E10-S1

## Description

Compute intent accuracy, per-category recall, escalation accuracy, structured-output compliance and grounding metrics, evaluate the vulnerable-customer safety set separately, and generate the AI evaluation report applying the 30-case rule per category and separate MOCK and LIVE sections. Establishes the baseline report in Slice 1.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Intent accuracy and per-category true positives, false negatives and recall are computed correctly on a fixture with known counts
2. A category with fewer than 30 labelled LIVE cases is reported as 'observation only' without a pass or fail claim and one with 30 or more shows pass or fail against 95 percent
3. Escalation accuracy is reported together with precision, recall and over-escalation rate
4. The report lists MOCK and LIVE in separate sections, and a MOCK result never appears in the LIVE section or in any accuracy claim
5. The vulnerable-customer safety set is evaluated separately from intent metrics, any missed escalation in it is reported as a critical policy violation, and no numeric vulnerable-customer performance percentage is published unless an explicit metric is defined and the set has at least 30 labelled LIVE cases
6. The 90 percent overall intent accuracy target is reported pass or fail only for LIVE runs and a MOCK run is never evaluated against it
7. The 95 percent recall claim rules are applied per category to FINANCIAL_HARDSHIP, DISPUTE, REQUEST_HUMAN and the other mandatory-escalation scenarios (exceptional arrangement, policy exception, settlement request), each with its own 30-case minimum
