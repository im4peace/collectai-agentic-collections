# E11-S2: Journey B1 end-to-end: eligible payment arrangement

- **ID:** E11-S2
- **Epic:** E11 - End-to-End Journey Verification
- **Layer:** Service
- **Slice:** 2
- **Group:** J
- **depends_on:** E7-S4, E6-S5, E9-S2

## Description

Automated end-to-end verification of Journey B1: PAYMENT_PLAN, deterministic eligibility, option selection, confirmation, arrangement creation and audit, including the no-eligibility and exceptional branches.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. A Playwright test as the bound CUSTOMER requests a payment plan and sees only the options returned by the eligibility service
2. Selecting an option and clicking Confirm creates exactly one ACTIVE arrangement even if Confirm is clicked twice
3. A no-eligible-option scenario tells the customer options are unavailable and offers a human, and an exceptional request scenario creates an escalation in COLLECTIONS_EXCEPTION_REVIEW with no approval language
4. The audit viewer shows the eligibility result, chosen option, confirmation and PolicyRuleSet version for the journey
