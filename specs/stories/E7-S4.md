# E7-S4: Exceptional arrangement handling and human decision

- **ID:** E7-S4
- **Epic:** E7 - Escalation and Human Review
- **Layer:** Service
- **Slice:** 2
- **Group:** I
- **depends_on:** E8-S1, E7-S2

## Description

Handle customer-requested arrangements that are outside eligible options as exceptional arrangements: escalate to human review, never state approval, never offer exceptional terms as available options, and let an authorized human approve or reject within policy authority (D-024). Depends on the arrangement conversation flow, not the reverse.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. A requested arrangement classified EXCEPTIONAL by the rules service creates an OPEN escalation with reason EXCEPTIONAL_ARRANGEMENT, stores the requested terms, and is routed to COLLECTIONS_EXCEPTION_REVIEW
2. The customer message says the request needs human review and contains no approval language such as 'approved' or 'we will grant' (guardrail check)
3. Exceptional terms never appear in the option list shown to customers by the AI
4. A human APPROVE of an exceptional arrangement is available only within the policy exception authority (exception type, thresholds and maximum overdue amount) and requires a reason, and REJECT requires a reason; both record the decision and an audit event
5. An approved exception creates the arrangement only through the domain service after revalidating authorization, policy exception authority and snapshot freshness, and a rejected exception creates no arrangement and informs the customer
