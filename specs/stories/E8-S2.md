# E8-S2: Financial hardship identification and handling

- **ID:** E8-S2
- **Epic:** E8 - Arrangements, Hardship and Disputes
- **Layer:** Service
- **Slice:** 2
- **Group:** I
- **depends_on:** E7-S2

## Description

Implement Journey B2: detect hardship from conversation, record structured indicators idempotently, suppress automated treatment, route a prioritized escalation to hardship review, and let a human decide, without approving or implying any relief or executing restructuring.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. A hardship message such as job loss produces a schema-valid HardshipCase with indicator types (for example JOB_LOSS) and customer statement
2. The AI reply contains no promise or implication of restructuring or relief (guardrail check passes on 20 scripted replies)
3. Automated recommendations and outreach for the account stop until a human decision, per the hardship suppression parameters of the PolicyRuleSet
4. A hardship escalation with reason FINANCIAL_HARDSHIP is routed to HARDSHIP_REVIEW with the elevated priority from the routing service and is visible on Customer 360 and in the queue
5. Hardship case creation is idempotent: the same idempotency key, or a repeated hardship message while a case is open, returns the existing case and creates no duplicate
6. A reviewer records the hardship decision with a mandatory reason, requests for restructuring or a policy exception are recorded as decisions or re-routed via ESCALATE with reason POLICY_EXCEPTION, and no restructuring or financial change is executed in the MVP
