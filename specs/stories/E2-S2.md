# E2-S2: PTP validation, payable amounts and satisfaction rule

- **ID:** E2-S2
- **Epic:** E2 - Deterministic Rules Engine
- **Layer:** Service
- **Slice:** 1
- **Group:** B
- **depends_on:** E1-S1, E1-S2

## Description

Implement deterministic validation for Promise-to-Pay amount and date against the injected Clock and PolicyRuleSet, the service that returns payable amounts for PAY_NOW, and the deterministic PTP satisfaction rule that tracks cumulative qualifying simulated payments. Provides reason codes and valid alternatives (AC-A3). The LLM never performs these calculations.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Amounts of zero, negative, over the overdue balance and with more than 2 decimals are rejected with reason codes ZERO_AMOUNT, NEGATIVE_AMOUNT, OVER_BALANCE and OVER_PRECISION
2. Dates in the past, and dates beyond the policy window (ptp_window_days from Clock now), are rejected with PAST_DATE and OUTSIDE_WINDOW; a date on the last permitted day is accepted
3. A rejection response includes at least one valid alternative amount or date range computed by the service
4. The payable-amounts function returns the overdue amount and the full outstanding balance as Decimal strings for an account and returns none for an account under suppression
5. The PTP satisfaction rule sums only successful simulated PaymentEvents at or above the qualifying minimum that occur on or before the due date and returns KEPT when the cumulative total is at least the promised amount, PENDING before the deadline otherwise, and BROKEN after the deadline
6. A single qualifying payment below the promised amount returns PENDING with cumulative_paid and remaining amounts as Decimal strings, and a second payment that brings the total to the promised amount returns KEPT
7. FAILED PaymentEvents, payments below the qualifying minimum and payments dated after the due date are excluded from the cumulative total
