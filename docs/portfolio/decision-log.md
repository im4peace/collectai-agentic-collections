# Product decision log (P4)

Portfolio deliverable P4. Every decision in the BRD decision log (`specs/brd/brd.md`, section 16) is recorded here with its status, the alternatives considered and the rationale. D-001 to D-029 were made in the BRD interview session; D-030 onward record material post-BRD decisions made during specification review. All BRD entries are dated 2026-09-21.

This file is maintained by hand. `backend/tests/portfolio/test_decision_log_doc.py` parses the BRD decision log and fails if any decision ID present there is missing from this file, so a new BRD decision can never be silently omitted (E10-S5 AC4).
